GINGERBOOKS - START HERE
========================
1. Double-click  GingerBooks.html  (opens in Chrome or Edge, no internet needed).
2. Read  Guide\Owner-Guide.html  for step-by-step instructions.
3. IMPORTANT: In the app, go to Settings & backup > "Choose auto-backup file"
   and save it inside the  Backups  folder. Your data is stored in the browser,
   so this backup file is your safety copy.

Folders
  Backups          - auto-backup and downloaded backup files (.json)
  Bank statements  - statements downloaded from net banking (.xlsx / .csv)
  Invoices         - invoice / credit note PDFs (Print > Save as PDF)
  Reports          - GST reports, P&L and other Excel (CSV) downloads
  Guide            - owner guide
